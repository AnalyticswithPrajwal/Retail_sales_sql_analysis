# Retail Sales SQL Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project demonstrates an end-to-end SQL data analysis workflow using a retail store sales dataset. It walks through the process of turning raw transaction data into business insights, focusing on data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and revenue optimization.

The project highlights:
* **Data cleaning and validation** to ensure structural integrity
* **Exploratory data analysis (EDA)** to understand baseline numbers
* **Business-focused SQL analysis** targeting core operational metrics
* **Customer and revenue analysis** to find high-value patterns
* **Advanced SQL** utilizing CTEs, window functions, and multi-level aggregations

---

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset contains **12,575 retail transactions** capturing granular point-of-sale data, including:
* Transaction and customer IDs
* Product categories and individual items
* Price, quantity, and total spending
* Payment methods and store locations
* Transaction dates and discount statuses

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Validation
To establish a reliable source of truth, the raw data was preserved separately while a cleaned table was built using the following steps:
* **Inspection:** Reviewing the raw dataset structure and categorical fields.
* **Completeness:** Checking for missing values and recovering them where possible.
* **Uniqueness:** Identifying and removing duplicate transactions.
* **Integrity Constraints:** Validating numerical ranges (e.g., negative prices/quantities) and invalid data.
* **Optimization:** Applying appropriate data types and primary/foreign key constraints.

## 💡 Business Analysis Questions Answered
The analysis script (`02_business_analysis.sql`) addresses key stakeholders' questions, grouped by focus area:

### 🪙 Revenue & High-Level Metrics
* What is the total revenue, total transactions, and total units sold?
* What is the average transaction value?
* How does revenue change month-over-month?

### 📦 Product & Inventory Performance
* Which product category generates the highest revenue?
* Which specific 10 items generate the most revenue?
* Which category has the highest quantity sold?
* Which category-location combination is the most profitable?

### 🏪 Store Operations & Logistics
* How does revenue compare across different store locations?
* Which payment method is used most frequently by customers?
* Do discounted transactions result in a significantly different average transaction value?

### 👥 Customer Behavior & Segmentation
* Who are the top 10 customers by total spending?
* What is the average number of transactions and amount spent per customer?
* **Pareto Analysis:** What percentage of total revenue is generated by the top 20% of customers?
